🌏 Travel Tips

  • Bring warm clothing, even during summer.
  • Enjoy fresh seafood at local markets.
  • Respect wildlife and keep a safe distance.
  • Stay on designated trails and boardwalks.
  • Check opening hours and transport schedules before visiting.
  • Book outdoor activities with experienced local guides.
